Six-sticker recognition lets you identify a PLL case by looking at just **the six stickers in the top rows of the front and right faces**. With practice, it can reduce the time you spend inspecting the cube and deciding which algorithm to use during PLL.

This tutorial is for cubers who are already **comfortable with all 21 PLL algorithms** and want to make their PLL recognition faster.
